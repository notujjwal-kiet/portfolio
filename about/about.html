I work across the layer between hardware and the people using it. Firmware on one end, interfaces on the other, and the uncomfortable glue in between.

What I actually do

I build systems that ship — AI research tooling, embedded flight controllers, terminal applications, and the occasional interface. The common thread is that all of them have to leave my laptop and work somewhere real.

Most of my time goes into understanding the problem well enough that the solution becomes obvious. The rest is the part nobody talks about: the debugging, the rewrites, the 3 a.m. realisation that the sensor was fine and the wiring was wrong.
